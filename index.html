How it works

1

The client asks for more

"While you're in there…" — extra cabinets, upgraded tile, a moved wall. It happens on every job. The question is whether it's in writing.

2

Price it in minutes

Add line items — quantity, your cost, your markup. ScopeAmend totals the change order and shows the new contract value. Your costs and markup stay on your screen only.

3

Get it signed first

Download a clean PDF with the work, the price, the schedule impact, and a client signature block. No signature, no extra work. That's the rule that saves your margin.

The fine print, in plain words

Terms

ScopeAmend provides document preparation and change-order management tools. It does not provide legal advice or guarantee the enforceability of any document. Users are responsible for ensuring that their agreements comply with applicable laws, licensing requirements, and original contract terms.

Privacy

Your projects, change orders, company info, and logo are stored only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing you type is uploaded to us or anyone else — there is no account and no tracking of your numbers. Export a backup file from the app regularly, because clearing your browser data erases what's saved here.

Billing & cancellation

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or the Stripe customer portal — no email to us required, no cancellation fee. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the free calculator keeps working either way.
